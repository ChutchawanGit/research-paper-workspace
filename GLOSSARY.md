# Research Paper Reading & Annotation Platform

A multi-user web platform for collecting, categorizing, reading, and annotating research PDF papers, built as a flagship developer portfolio product for general users (students, researchers, and technical readers).

## Language

**User**:
An authenticated individual (student, researcher, or reader) who owns a private Library, uploads papers, and creates annotations.
_Avoid_: Member, client, visitor

**Library**:
The personal workspace and document repository belonging to a specific User. By default, completely private and inaccessible to other users.
_Avoid_: Workspace, vault, repository

**Paper**:
A research publication document in PDF format along with its bibliographic metadata (title, authors, publication date, abstract) owned by a User's Library.
_Avoid_: File, document, item

**Collection**:
A user-defined thematic group within a Library used to categorize Papers.
_Avoid_: Folder, category, directory

**Tag**:
A flexible keyword label attached to a Paper for cross-cutting filtering.
_Avoid_: Label, topic

**Highlight**:
A text selection on a specific PDF page, stored with page index, text excerpt, and normalized spatial coordinates.
_Avoid_: Marker, underline

**Note**:
A structured Markdown reading note or analytical commentary linked to a Paper or Highlight.
_Avoid_: Comment, memo, scratchpad

**Share Link**:
A cryptographically secure, unguessable URL generated by the owner for a specific Paper, granting unauthenticated read-only access to view the PDF and selected notes without logging in.
_Avoid_: Public link, export link, invite
